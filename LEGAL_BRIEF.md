## 2. What We Need From You

We require the following legal documents and advice, in priority order:

### Priority 1 — Privacy Policy
We collect subscriber names, email addresses, payment details, and platform usage data. We require a compliant Privacy Policy under the **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**. The policy should cover:
- What personal data is collected and why
- How it is stored and protected
- Whether it is shared with third parties (payment processor, analytics)
- User rights to access and correct their data
- Contact details for privacy enquiries

### Priority 2 — Subscription Agreement (Clickwrap)
We require a binding Subscription Agreement that subscribers must actively accept at sign-up (checkbox or "I Accept" button). This agreement should cover:

- Grant of licence (personal, non-transferable, per subscriber or per organisation)
- Permitted use: internal reports, board papers, grant applications, policy submissions, presentations
- Prohibited use: redistribution, resale, sharing outputs with third parties outside the organisation, scraping, use in competing products
- Intellectual property: all platform content, synthesis, visualisations, and AI-generated analysis owned by Impact Analytics Australia
- Payment and subscription terms (including renewal, cancellation, refund policy)
- Liability exclusion — consistent with Australian Consumer Law mandatory guarantees
- Termination rights (for non-payment, breach, misuse)
- Governing law clause

### Priority 3 — Review of Existing Terms & Legal Page
We have a draft Terms & Legal page currently live at the platform URL `/legal`. We require you to:
- Review all 8 clauses for enforceability under Australian law
- Ensure liability exclusions are correctly worded to survive ACL scrutiny
- Confirm IP ownership clauses are adequate
- Advise on any gaps or unenforceable provisions
- Update the governing law / jurisdiction clause to reflect our registered state

## 3. Current Draft Terms (for your review)

The current `/legal` page contains the following 8 clauses:

1. **Intellectual Property** — asserts ownership of platform design, synthesis, visualisations, and AI content; acknowledges underlying source copyright with ABS, AIHW, AHURI etc.

2. **Subscription Licence** — personal, non-transferable licence; defines permitted use (internal reports, submissions, briefings); prohibits redistribution, resale, scraping, and use in competing products without written consent.

3. **Attribution** — recommends citing underlying source alongside HIVE (e.g. "Source: ABS Cat. 8731.0, via HIVE Intelligence").

4. **No Professional Advice** — content for informational purposes only; not financial, legal, investment, or professional advice; subscribers responsible for independent verification.

5. **Accuracy & Data Currency** — no warranty on completeness, accuracy, or timeliness; underlying data updated periodically and may not reflect most recent government release.

6. **Limitation of Liability** — excludes all liability for direct, indirect, consequential loss to the maximum extent permitted by Australian law.

7. **Governing Law** — currently drafted as New South Wales; to be updated to reflect actual state of operation.

8. **Contact & Licensing Enquiries** — directs to Impact Analytics Australia website.

## 4. Key Questions for the Solicitor

1. Are the liability exclusion clauses in the current draft enforceable under the Australian Consumer Law, given the ACL's non-excludable consumer guarantees? What language is needed to make them hold?

2. Does our use of publicly available ABS, AIHW, and AHURI data within a paid subscription product create any copyright exposure? Are there any licence conditions attached to those datasets we need to comply with?

3. What is the minimum required Privacy Policy content given our data collection practices? Are we caught by the Privacy Act if we have fewer than 250 employees?

4. For the clickwrap agreement — what is the legally sufficient mechanism in Australia to establish that a subscriber has accepted the terms? (checkbox, email confirmation, etc.)

5. Should the governing law clause reference a specific state, or "Australia" generally? What is the practical implication for enforcement?

6. Are there any sector-specific considerations for a platform used by government-funded community housing providers in submissions to government agencies?
